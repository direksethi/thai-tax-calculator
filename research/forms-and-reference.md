# Forms and reference

Compiled 2026-11-04. Primary sources are the Revenue Department's own PDFs (forms and filing instructions for tax year 2568, and the first 2569 forms), which were downloaded and text-extracted. Every rd.go.th URL in this file was checked with an HTTP range request on 2026-11-04 and returned `206 application/pdf` unless marked otherwise. News sources are used only for 2569 measures that are not yet on an RD form.

Conventions: B.E. years (2568 = 2025, 2569 = 2026, 2570 = 2027). "TY" means tax year (calendar year of income). "e-Filing +8" means the 8-day extension for online filing (see section B.9).

---

### B.13 Exact rules for items 20, 21, 22

**Item 20, new home construction** (Ministerial Regulation 393 (2567), DG Notification 445, 24 Jun 2567):
- Exemption of 10,000 for every full 1,000,000 of construction cost paid to a VAT-registered contractor, max 100,000, one house only. Formula: `min(100,000, floor(cost / 1,000,000) x 10,000)`.
- Contract made and construction started 9 Apr 2567 to 31 Dec 2568; stamp duty paid electronically; full tax invoice (s.86/4) naming construction cost; separate land and construction contracts; contractor must not be a property developer subject to SBT; new build only (no extensions or renovations).
- Claimed in the tax year construction is completed (later of contract end date, permit or notification). So TY2569 returns can still include it (it remains item 18 on the 2569 PND 94).
- Co-contractors split equally; spouses with separate filing each get up to 100,000; combined filing up to 100,000 each.
- Evidence: tax invoice, construction contract, building permit or notification.

**Item 21, art purchases** (Ministerial Regulation 400 (2568), DG Notification 465, 26 Dec 2568):
- It is "ค่าซื้องานศิลปะสาขาทัศนศิลป์": visual art purchases. Actual amount, max 100,000 per tax year.
- Purchases 1 Jan 2568 to 31 Dec 2570, in Thailand, from: a National Artist (visual arts), a Silpathorn artist (visual arts), or an artist registered with a state agency named by the DG; or from a company, juristic partnership, or a public-charity foundation or association (s.47(7)(b)) that sells or auctions works created by those artists.
- Visual art means 2D or 3D: painting, sculpture, printmaking, photography, mixed media.
- Evidence: full tax invoice (VAT seller) or s.105 receipt (non-VAT seller) naming the buyer, plus a certified description: artist name, title, type, year, size.
- The same purchase cannot be used for any other exemption.

**Item 22, domestic travel 29 Oct to 15 Dec 2568** (Ministerial Regulation 401 (2568), DG Notification 463, 19 Dec 2568; RD Q&A https://www.rd.go.th/fileadmin/user_upload/lorkhor/newsbanner/2025/10/PR22102568.pdf):
- Eligible spend: hotel, certified Thai homestay, registered non-hotel lodging, and restaurant bills (food, drinks including alcohol, service charge; no stay required) paid to VAT-registered businesses. Lodging must be stayed in within the window.
- Evidence: full tax invoice with the taxpayer's ID number, date of stay or meal, and province (plus the non-hotel registration number).
- Spend cap: 20,000 of actual spend in total. The first 10,000 may be on paper or e-Tax Invoice; the next 10,000 must be e-Tax Invoice.
- Multiplier: 1x for main cities (22.1), 1.5x for secondary-tourism provinces (22.2): 55 whole provinces plus named districts in 15 provinces (list in the Q&A, question 3).
- Maximum deduction: 20,000 if all main city; 30,000 if all secondary city (RD infographic: "ไม่เกิน 30,000 บาท").
- Allocation (RD worked example, Q&A question 2): fill the 10,000 paper-or-e-Tax bucket from paper invoices first, the extra 10,000 bucket from e-Tax invoices only; within each bucket count secondary-city spend first. Example: secondary paper 6,000, secondary e-Tax 4,000, main paper 5,000, main e-Tax 7,000 gives paper bucket 1.5 x 6,000 + 1 x 4,000 = 13,000 and e-Tax bucket 1.5 x 4,000 + 1 x 6,000 = 12,000, total 25,000.
- Calculator rule (maximising): bucket A (cap 10,000) takes paper spend, secondary before main, then tops up from e-Tax spend if room remains; bucket B (cap 10,000) takes remaining e-Tax spend, secondary before main. Deduction = sum of (secondary x 1.5 + main x 1.0).
